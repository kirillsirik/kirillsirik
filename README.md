I am a B.S.E. candidate in Operations Research and Financial Engineering at Princeton University, with minors in Quantitative Economics and in Statistics and Machine Learning.

My research interests lie at the intersection of statistics, econometrics, and operations research. I study estimation and inference when models are only approximately correct and data are limited, and I want to understand which guarantees still hold in finite samples and how to report uncertainty when it informs decisions.

**Research interests**

- Econometric theory and microeconometrics
- Statistical learning in high dimensions
- Semiparametric and causal inference
- Nonasymptotic estimation and uncertainty quantification
- Statistical decision theory
